### Diff-scope review (inline, narrowed)

- **F8. Native-control assumptions were wrong three times and only the running app showed it.** The 3-4 spec assumed `listRowBackground` would remove the macOS blue highlight and that the existing `HSplitView` fix still held. The author reported the list width still changed, arrow keys did not move selection, and the blue remained (`spec-3-4-…md`, Implementation Notes, first and second feedback), then that white plus ring was not visible enough (third). Five commits (`4d473d4`, `5a55d9d`, `845f2d0`, `bce207f`, `51b245b`) resulted. The four review layers ran after those rounds and added one real improvement (`JournalSelection.next` in Core, 7 tests seen failing first) and recorded two decisions. This is retro 2's A6 working: the running-app check came before review in 3-4. Disposition: accept; process lesson (B6): when a story depends on a native control's look or keys (`List`, split view), the spec's plan should be "try in the running app first" with the assumption written as unverified, not as a step.
- **F9. Selection moved off `List(selection:)`, so native list behavior is gone.** The review lists lost Return/Space, Home/End/PageUp/PageDown, type-select, a visible focus ring (`.focusEffectDisabled()`, line 79) and modified arrows, and rejected the point as low for a single-selection journal (`spec-3-4-…md`, Rejected). Also: a stale `selection` after a filter change shows the placeholder, and clicks in the row inset margins do not select. Disposition: accept (author-requested outcome), keep in the record so Epic 5 does not rediscover it.
- **F10. VoiceOver selection of a Journal row is unverified.** The row uses `onTapGesture`, `.accessibilityElement(children: .ignore)` and the `isButton`/`isSelected` traits with no `accessibilityAction` (`JournalEntriesView.swift:~238-241`). Two deferrals ask for the same check (3-3: row trait may be dropped; 3-4: VO+Space may not select). Neither is closed in `deferred-work.md`. Because `List(selection:)` was removed after the 3-3 deferral, the 3-3 concern now applies to hand-built traits. Story 3.3 AC "VoiceOver reads the note together with the duration" depends on it. Disposition: **fix now** (B3): the author runs VoiceOver once; if VO+Space does not select, add an `accessibilityAction`.
- **F11. Contrast of meta text on the selected fill.** `text-secondary` on `surface-selected` measures 3.83:1 (below AA). The review chose `text-primary` for text on a selected row (`JournalEntriesView.swift:246`, `DESIGN.md:151`). The running row (`surface-inset`) was not measured in the record. Disposition: accept for `surface-selected`; **defer** the `surface-inset` measurement to Epic 5's whole-app check (`5-4`).
- **F12. Accepted edge cases, recorded so later retros stop re-flagging them:** midnight rollover keeps the old «today» group growing until the next reload (`deferred-work.md`, review of 3-2); a selected running row shows no selection cue (decision (c)); the day total splits a midnight-spanning entry by day while its row shows the full duration (spec 3-2, planning decision).
