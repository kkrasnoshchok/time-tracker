## Findings

### Spec-to-implementation reconciliation

- **F1. Sprint status lags the epic.** `sprint-status.yaml` has `epic-2: in-progress` although `2-1..2-4` are `done` (`development_status`, epic-2 block). Epic 1 had the same drift (retro 1, F1) and A1 fixed the story keys, not the epic key. Disposition: **fix now** (A3). Prevention: the step that closes the last story should flip the epic key.
- **F2. Story 2.4 AC1 is not met as written.** AC1 requires screenshots of idle, typing, running and prompt states (`epics.md`, Story 2.4). The spec records none: "the ticks rest on that statement only" (`spec-2-4-…md`, Author confirmation). The review chose to relabel the section "author-reported" rather than get evidence (`spec-2-4-…md`, Review Findings, Decision). Disposition: **fix now** (A1): attach screenshots or one-line results per item, or amend the AC to say author-reported. Prevention: a story that hands manual checks to the author should ask for one line per item at the moment the author reports.
- **F3. The rest of Epic 2 matches its ACs on the evidence.**
  - 2.1: opaque `surfaceCard` panel, `Radius.panel`, palette hairline ring, 60pt field, no material or shadow (`App/Palette/PaletteView.swift:30-64`; `Space.paletteFieldHeight == 60` pinned in `DesignTokenTests`).
  - 2.2: `CategoryResolver` (58 lines) with 155 lines of tests seen failing first; `viewModel.input` stays the source of truth.
  - 2.3: `PaletteSelection` (29 lines) + 94 lines of tests; `Radius.row` 10 and `Space.suggestionRowHeight` 40 pinned; prompts via `ErrorStrip`; `PalettePanel` uses no show/hide animation (`PalettePanelTests`).
  - 2.4: `App/Palette` in `scripts/token-guard-paths.txt` (tail of file), guard exit 0.
  - Core untouched: `git diff --stat 83a143b..f648064 -- Packages` is empty.
  Disposition: accept.
- **F4. Accepted deviation: first-word resolution and selection logic live in `App/`, not Core.** CLAUDE.md says all logic lives in `Packages/TimeTrackerCore`; the epic constraint explicitly allowed an `App/` resolver because Core's `PaletteViewModel` exposes only `input` and `items` (`epic-2-context.md`, Requirements). The 2.2 review recorded the move to Core as "a Story 2.4+ design call" and nobody took it (`spec-2-2-…md`, Rejected). Disposition: **accept** for now, raise as an open question (Q1).
- **F5. Four frozen specs describe the work slightly differently than the diff.** 2.1 says "Changed only `PaletteView.swift`" but `PalettePanel` and `PaletteController` also changed (`bce4581`); 2.2's frozen Approach names `onKeyPress` for Backspace, replaced by the invisible-anchor fix (`5881471`); 2.3 lists `PaletteController.swift` as changed but it was not. The reviews rejected these because the fix edits a frozen block. Disposition: accept (the commits are the record).

### Duplication map / pattern divergence

- **F6. Tag-muting logic is a static function inside the view.** `PaletteView.mutingTags(in:)` is tested from `AppTests/CategoryResolverTests.swift:73` but lives in `PaletteView.swift`, while the other pure helpers (`CategoryResolver`, `PaletteSelection`) got their own files. Divergent placement; Epic 3's Journal rows may want the same muting. Disposition: **defer** (A5).
- **F7. Guard gaps remain.** `.fontWeight(.light)` on the magnifier is an author-approved exception to the 400/500 rule (`spec-2-1-…md`, Decision) that no guard pattern could see, and the 2.4 review deferred `Color.white`/`.black`, `NSFont`, `Font.custom`, `.system(.title)` (`deferred-work.md`, review of 2-4). `grep` over `App/Palette` finds none today. Disposition: **defer** until Epic 3 adds screens (A4); same lesson as epic 1 A3 (widen before the next screens enter the list).
- No duplicated logic found between stories: tones and spacing come from `App/Design` (`Color.textPrimary`, `surfaceCard`, `surfaceCanvas`, `Radius.*`, `Space.*`); `grep` finds no `Date()` and no hex in `App/Palette`.

### God-class / size growth

- `App/Palette/PaletteView.swift` is the churn leader: +223/-87 over 8 of 12 commits, now 267 lines (`git_evidence.py`, `wc -l`). It holds the panel shell, input row, token layer, tag muting, running row, suggestion rows, prompts and submit logic. Not a god class yet, but every Palette story touched it and Epic 3/4 will copy its patterns. Disposition: **defer** with F6 (A5): split rows/prompts into subviews before the next epic edits it.
- Other files stay small: `CategoryResolver` 58, `PalettePanel` 46, `PaletteController` 33, `PaletteSelection` 29.

### Diff-scope review (inline, narrowed)

- **F8. The one real bug of the epic was found by the author, not by review or tests.** Backspace never removed the token because the `TextField` consumes Backspace, so `onKeyPress(.delete)` never fired (`spec-2-2-…md`, Post-review fix; fix `5881471`). Three review layers and the unit tests could not see it; it needed the running app. Disposition: accept (fixed), process lesson (A6): for behavior that depends on the text field or key handling, schedule the manual run before the review, not after.
- **F9. Deferred items closed only on the author's word, and `deferred-work.md` does not show it.** The 2.1/2.2/2.4 deferred items (hotkey race, dark-mode caret, stale `TextSelection`, VoiceOver, tag drift, leading whitespace) are ticked in the 2.4 checklist, but `deferred-work.md` still lists them as open and has no "Resolved in epic 2" block (it has one for epic 1: line 56). Disposition: **fix now** (A2).
- **F10. Swift-concept explanations are owed.** Epic ACs for 2.2 and 2.3 and the CLAUDE.md rule require TypeScript/React analogies for `@Bindable`, `@FocusState`, `onKeyPress`, `@ViewBuilder`, `accessibilityReduceMotion`, `NSHostingView`. The specs say "explained afterwards" and no artifact records it; the 2.4 review rejected the point as outside the story. No evidence found that it happened. Disposition: **fix now** (A7): run `explain-change` over the range.
